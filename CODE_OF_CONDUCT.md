# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the GullyCaster community a harassment-free experience for everyone–regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

### Unacceptable Behavior
- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Project-Specific Guidelines

### 1. Prioritize Low-End Devices
Our target users often have mid-tier Android phones. Code contributions that unnecessarily increase RAM usage, add heavy animation libraries, or require flagship hardware will not be merged.

### 2. Respect Regional Diversity
GullyCaster serves communities across South Asia. All feature discussions must consider:
- **Urdu (RTL)** - Right-to-left layout support
- **Hindi** - Devanagari script support
- **Bangla** - Bengali script support

Do not dismiss localization concerns as "minor" or "later work."

### 3. Thermal Efficiency
Live streaming generates heat. Contributions should consider battery life and thermal throttling on budget devices.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
